struktur sistem urinaria

1. General Anatomical Position
> Spine (vertebral column): Used as an orientation landmark.
The kidneys are located on both sides of the spine in the lower back region.
2. Kidneys
>Right kidney
>Left kidney
Basic kidney structure:
Cortex: The outer layer where initial filtration occurs.
Medulla: The inner region containing the renal pyramids.
Renal pelvis: Funnel-shaped structure that collects urine.
3. Adrenal glands: Located on top of each kidney.
4. Renal tubules – Magnified Inset
Microscopic structural units of the kidney responsible for urine formation.
5. Ureters: >Right ureter
>Left ureter
Functions:
1.Transport urine from the kidneys to the bladder.
2.Use peristaltic movements to propel urine.
6. Bladder
>Functions:
Temporarily stores urine.
Expands as it fills.
>Main muscle layer:
Detrusor muscle: Contracts during urination.
7. Urethra
Function: The final passageway for urine excretion.
